Led Manager
Streamlined framework for selecting, editing, and deploying LED animations for embedded controllers.
- Define reusable color palettes and timing presets for common lighting scenarios.
- Provide a visual timeline editor for sequencing effects across multiple zones.
- Export device-ready configurations to popular LED firmware formats.
- Maintain a library of shareable lighting patterns with tagging and versioning.
- Include a lightweight simulator to preview animations before hardware deployment.
